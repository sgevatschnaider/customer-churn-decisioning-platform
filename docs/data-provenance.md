Blocking thresholds are intentionally stricter than observed values but allow isolated source corrections: duplicates above 5% and negative prices above 0.1% fail validation.

## Privacy

`CustomerID` is an operational source identifier, not a named identity, but it is still treated as linkable. Raw and processed datasets are ignored. The committed retention queue replaces identifiers with stable, repository-specific SHA-256 prefixes. No descriptions, contact information, secrets, or sensitive personal attributes are published.

## Synthetic fixture

`data/fixtures/transactions.csv` is generated by `generate_fixture(seed=42)`. IDs begin with `SYN`, descriptions explicitly say synthetic, and purchasing patterns are deterministic. It exists only for tests and CI and must never be cited as evidence of business performance.
